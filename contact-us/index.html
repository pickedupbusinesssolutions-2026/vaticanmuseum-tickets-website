Frequently Asked Visitor Questions

Need a fast answer before writing to us? Check our most frequently visited planning guides:

🎟️ How early do tickets sell out?

Official general tickets sell out minutes after release 30 days in advance. If dates are full, check our ticket alternatives guide for verified guided tours.

⏰ What are the opening hours?

The Colosseum opens daily at 08:30, with closing hours varying by season between 16:30 (winter) and 19:15 (summer). See full details in our opening hours guide.

🎒 Can I bring large backpacks?

No. Large suitcases, heavy backpacks, and glass containers are strictly prohibited, and there is no cloakroom on site. Check our bag regulations guide before you go.
