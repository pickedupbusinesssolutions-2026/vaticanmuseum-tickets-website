📋 Essential Visitor Tips

What You Need to Know Before Visiting

📅

When to Book

Book at least 2 to 4 weeks ahead for travel between April and October. Early morning slots (8:00 AM) and evening tours sell out especially quickly.

👔

Dress Code Rules

Shoulders and knees must be covered. Sleeveless shirts, crop tops, short shorts, and hats are strictly forbidden across the museums and basilica.

👟

Walking Route

The standard one-way route is 3 to 4 km long. Wear comfortable shoes and take advantage of the free water fountains in the courtyards.

🛆

Free Cloakroom

Large backpacks, bags over 40×35×15 cm, tripods, and long umbrellas must be checked into the free cloakroom at the Viale Vaticano entrance.

💡
Pro Tip: Arrive 15 Minutes Before Your Timed Slot

Security screening operates efficiently with metal detectors, but queues form at peak hours. Arrive 15 minutes before your ticket time to ensure prompt entry.
