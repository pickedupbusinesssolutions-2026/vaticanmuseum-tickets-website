Vatican Museum Entrance
Quick answer: The Vatican Museums have one main visitor entrance, on Viale Vaticano, along the northern side of the Vatican walls. The nearest metro is Ottaviano or Cipro on Line A, about a 7 to 10 minute walk, and bus 49 stops right in front. It is not the same as the St. Peter's Basilica entrance, which is off St. Peter's Square, a 10 to 15 minute walk around the walls. There is no separate Sistine Chapel entrance, you reach the chapel by walking the museum route from this same entrance. Everyone passes through airport-style security on the way in.

The entrance is on Viale Vaticano, postcode 00165, the road that runs along the northern wall of Vatican City. Directions here
You cannot miss it: it is a large, curved monumental doorway set into the wall, with statues of Michelangelo and Raphael above it. There is a Caffè Vaticano and a line of ticket and tour desks along the street just before it.
This is the one and only public entrance to the museums. Whether you have a self-guided ticket, a guided tour, or you are buying on the day, everyone comes in through this door on Viale Vaticano.
How to Find the Entrance
The entrance is easy to reach by public transport, and our how to get to the Vatican guide has the full directions, including by car. In short:
* By metro: Take Line A to Ottaviano (signed Ottaviano-Musei Vaticani) or Cipro. Both are about a 7 to 10 minute walk. From Ottaviano, walk down Via Ottaviano to the walls, then turn right and follow the wall uphill to the entrance.
* By bus: The number 49 stops in the square right in front of the entrance. Lines 32, 81, and 982 stop at Piazza del Risorgimento, about a 5 minute walk away, and tram 19 stops there too.
* By taxi: There is a taxi rank in the square in front of the entrance.
* On foot from St. Peter's Square: follow the Vatican wall north and uphill for about 10 to 15 minutes until you reach the entrance on Viale Vaticano.
The Entrance, the Exit, and St. Peter's
Three doors trip people up, so here is how they differ.
The entrance is on Viale Vaticano, where you come in. The exit is a separate door further along the same street, where the famous spiral staircase leads you out at the end of your visit. You do not leave the way you came in, so do not arrange to meet anyone back at the entrance.
The St. Peter's Basilica entrance is a different place entirely. The basilica is entered from St. Peter's Square, not from Viale Vaticano, and it is a 10 to 15 minute walk around the walls. 
The exception is on a guided tour, which can use an internal shortcut from the Sistine Chapel straight into the basilica. Our St. Peter's Basilica tickets guide explains how to visit it on your own.
Is there a separate Sistine Chapel Entrance?
No, and this is the most common question. You cannot enter the Sistine Chapel directly. The chapel is the last room of the Vatican Museums, so you reach it by coming in through the main entrance on Viale Vaticano and walking the museum route to the end, past the Gallery of Maps and the Raphael Rooms. 
Any Vatican Museums ticket includes the chapel, and there is no chapel-only door or ticket. Our Sistine Chapel tickets guide covers how the visit works.
Security and Bags
Everyone goes through airport-style security just inside the entrance, with metal detectors, even if you have a skip-the-line ticket. Leave extra time at the gate in busy periods.
Large items are not allowed in the galleries. Bags, backpacks, and cases larger than about 40 by 35 by 15 cm must go into the free cloakroom near the entrance, along with tripods, long umbrellas, and sharp objects. Travel light and you will clear security faster. 
There is also a step-free priority route for visitors with mobility needs, which our wheelchair and disability access guide covers.
How to Skip the Entrance Queue
There are two lines at the entrance: the regular walk-up queue for people buying tickets on the day, and the priority lane for visitors who booked a timed ticket in advance. The walk-up queue can run one to two hours in peak season, while the priority lane is far quicker.
The simple fix is to book a timed skip-the-line entry ticket before you arrive, so you join the shorter lane. Aim to get to the entrance about 15 to 20 minutes before your slot to clear security.
Frequently Asked Questions
Where is the Vatican Museums entrance?
On Viale Vaticano, postcode 00165, along the northern wall of Vatican City. It is a large curved doorway with statues of Michelangelo and Raphael above it, near the Caffè Vaticano. It is the only public entrance to the museums.
Is the Vatican Museum entrance the same as St. Peter's Basilica?
No. The museum entrance is on Viale Vaticano. St. Peter's Basilica is entered from St. Peter's Square, a 10 to 15 minute walk around the walls. They are two separate places.
Where is the Sistine Chapel entrance?
There is no separate entrance. The Sistine Chapel is the last room of the Vatican Museums, so you enter through the main museum entrance on Viale Vaticano and walk the route to reach it.
What is the nearest metro to the Vatican Museums entrance?
The nearest metro to the Vatican Museums entrance is Ottaviano (Ottaviano-Musei Vaticani) or Cipro, both on Line A, each about a 7 to 10 minute walk. Bus 49 stops right in front of the entrance.
Do I exit the same way I came in?
No. The exit is a separate door further along Viale Vaticano, with the famous spiral staircase, so you leave at a different point from where you entered.
How early should I arrive at the entrance?
About 15 to 20 minutes before your timed slot, to allow for security. In peak season the queue can build before the 8:00 am opening, so for an early slot it helps to be there ahead of time.
