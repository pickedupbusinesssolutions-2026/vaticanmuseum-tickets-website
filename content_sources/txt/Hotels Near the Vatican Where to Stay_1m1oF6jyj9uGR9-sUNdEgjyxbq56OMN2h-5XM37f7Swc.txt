The Best Areas to Stay
Four neighbourhoods sit around the Vatican, and each has a different feel:
* Borgo: The strip right next to St. Peter's, including pretty Borgo Pio. It is the closest you can stay to the Basilica, a few minutes on foot, and the most atmospheric, though the restaurants right on it are touristy.
* Prati: A smart, residential area a short walk north, with elegant streets, good shops, and some of the best food near the Vatican. A great all-round base, about a 10 to 20 minute walk to the Museums.
* Ottaviano and Cipro: The busier streets around the two metro stops, handy for the Museums entrance and for quick trips across the city on Metro Line A. Our how to get to the Vatican guide covers the lines.
* Aurelia: Further out and up the hill, quieter and cheaper, about a 15 minute walk or a short bus ride. Good value if you do not mind being a bit removed.
For most visitors, Borgo is best for being closest to St. Peter's, and Prati is best for the overall experience.
Luxury Hotels Near the Vatican
* Villa Agrippina Gran Meliá is the standout five-star, with landscaped grounds and an outdoor pool, which is rare in central Rome.
* Le Méridien Visconti is a polished four-star between the Vatican and the Tiber, a couple of minutes from Castel Sant'Angelo and a short walk to St. Peter's.
* Hotel dei Consoli Vaticano is an upscale four-star in Prati with a rooftop terrace looking across to St. Peter's dome.
Mid-Range Hotels Near the Vatican
* Hotel Sant'Anna is a traditional, family-friendly three-star in Borgo, a short walk from the Basilica.
* Hotel della Conciliazione sits on Borgo Pio, moments from St. Peter's, with its own restaurant.
* Isa Hotel and Orazio Palace are comfortable four-stars in Prati, both with rooftop terraces, around a 15 to 20 minute walk.
* Trianon Aparthotel Vatican in Borgo Pio offers self-catering apartments, good for families or longer stays.
Budget Stays and Guesthouses
* Roma dei Papi is a small boutique-style hotel near Castel Sant'Angelo, a few minutes from the Basilica, at a gentler price.
* Hotel Paolo II and Bloom Hotel in the Aurelia area are quieter three-stars with lower rates, about a 15 minute walk.
* Convents and religious guesthouses are the classic budget option near the Vatican. They are simple and cheap, often with a curfew, and fill fast in busy periods, so book well ahead.
* B&Bs and apartments in Prati or a little further out can also work out cheaper than a hotel, especially for group sharing.
Best for an Early Museum Visit
If your main goal is to be first into the Museums, stay as close to the entrance as you can. Hotel Museum is a simple three-star just a couple of minutes from the Museums entrance, with 24-hour reception, and hotels around Ottaviano and Cipro are also within an easy walk of the door.
Being close only helps if you have a ticket ready, so pair an early start with a timed skip-the-line entry ticket for the first slot of the day. Our best time to visit guide explains why that early slot is worth it.
Tips for Booking

How close can I stay to St. Peter's?
Hotels in Borgo and on Borgo Pio are just a few minutes' walk from the Basilica. Some three-stars, like the Hotel Museum, are only a couple of minutes from the Museum entrance.
Are there budget hotels near the Vatican?
Yes. Simple three-stars in Aurelia, small boutique hotels like Roma dei Papi, convent and religious guesthouses, and B&Bs all offer cheaper stays within walking distance or a short bus ride.
Is it worth staying near the Vatican?
It is if the Vatican is a big part of your trip, as you can reach the Museums at opening and enjoy a calmer base. The trade-off is being further from the Colosseum and the historic centre, so it suits Vatican-focused visits best.
What is the nearest metro for Vatican hotels?
Ottaviano and Cipro on Metro Line A both serve the Vatican area, so a hotel near either is handy for the Museums and for trips across the city.
How far in advance should I book a hotel near the Vatican?
As early as you can. The area is in demand and fills up in peak season and around major religious events, so booking well ahead gets you better rooms and rates. Choose free cancellation to stay flexible.
