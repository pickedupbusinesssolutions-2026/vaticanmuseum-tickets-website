Which Vatican Gardens Tour to Choose
Both see the gardens and both include the museums, so the choice is about how you want to do it.
* Take the minibus if you want a shorter, easier visit, you would rather explore the museums at your own pace afterward, or you want the option to add Castel Sant'Angelo. Just note it is not for children under 6.
* Take the walking tour if you want a live guide the whole way, or you want to get closer to the fountains on foot. It also works for families with young children.
If the gardens are not a must and you mainly want the museums, a standard Vatican Museums guided tour or a self-guided skip-the-line entry ticket may suit you better.
How to Visit the Vatican Gardens
The gardens are the private grounds behind the Vatican, and they are not open for you to wander. Access is only ever on an organised tour with an escort, so a ticket to the gardens always means booking one of two tour types.
* The minibus tour - You ride through the gardens on an open-air minibus with an audio guide, staying seated for the drive.
* The walking tour - A live guide walks you through the gardens on foot.
Both then lead into the Vatican Museums and the Sistine Chapel, so a gardens ticket is really a combined ticket. 
What You See in the Vatican Gardens
The gardens cover more than 57 acres, nearly half of Vatican City, and both tours take you past the highlights.
* The Governor's Palace, the seat of Vatican City's administration, with flowerbeds shaped into the papal coat of arms.
* The Fountain of the Eagle, a grand 17th-century Baroque fountain built for Pope Paul V.
* The Grotto of Our Lady of Lourdes, a replica of the French sanctuary that popes use for quiet prayer.
* The Vatican Radio station and the papal heliport, the working modern side of the grounds.
* The Leonine Walls, the medieval defensive walls that once protected the Holy See.
After the gardens, you carry on into the museums and the Sistine Chapel, which both tickets include.
Meeting Point and How It Works
Both tours check in at the same place: the supplier office, Touristation Cappella Sistina, at Viale Vaticano 95, right across from the Vatican Museums entrance. Report about 20 minutes before your start time, as late arrivals cannot be fitted into the Vatican's timed slots.
On the minibus tour, staff escort you past security into the gardens, and the bus sets off about 15 minutes after you enter. If you are coming from elsewhere in Rome, see our how to get to the Vatican guide.
Dress Code, Rules, and Tips
The gardens are consecrated papal property, so the usual Vatican rules apply.
* Dress code is enforced - Shoulders and knees must be covered, for men and women. No sleeveless tops, shorts, or short skirts, or you will be turned away at security. See the Vatican dress code guide.
* Security applies to everyone - You pass through airport-style checks before entering the grounds.
* Travel light - Large backpacks, suitcases, and tripods are not allowed. Since the walking tour ends at St. Peter's, avoid leaving bags at the museum cloakroom or you face a long walk back to collect them.
* Photography - Allowed in the gardens and museums without flash, but not allowed at all inside the Sistine Chapel.
* Wear comfortable shoes for the walking tour, as the paths include cobbles, ramps, and stairs.
* Castel Sant'Angelo add-on - If you pick the €92 minibus option, you can use the Castel Sant'Angelo ticket afterward. It is a 10 to 15 minute walk down Via della Conciliazione.
Frequently Asked Questions
Are the Vatican Gardens free?
No. The gardens are private papal grounds, and the only way in is a paid tour, either the minibus ride or a guided walk. Both tickets also include the Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel.
Can you visit the Vatican Gardens on your own?
No. You cannot walk in independently. Access is always with an organised tour and an escort, since the gardens are not open to the general public.
How do you visit the Vatican Gardens?
Book either the open-air minibus tour with an audio guide or a guided walking tour on foot. Both start near the Vatican Museums entrance and lead into the museums afterward.
How long is the Vatican Gardens tour?
The minibus drive itself is about 45 minutes, and the whole visit including the museums runs about 3.5 to 4 hours. The walking tour is about 3 to 3.5 hours.
Are the Vatican Gardens tours wheelchair accessible?
No. Neither the minibus nor the walking tour is wheelchair accessible. The minibus needs you to step in and out on your own, and the walk includes stairs and ramps.
Can children join the Vatican Gardens tour?
Children of all ages can join the walking tour. Children under 6 are not allowed on the minibus for seatbelt safety reasons.
