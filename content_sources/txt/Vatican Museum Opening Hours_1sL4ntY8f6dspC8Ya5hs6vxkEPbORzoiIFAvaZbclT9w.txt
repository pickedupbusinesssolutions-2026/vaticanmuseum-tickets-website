Vatican Museum Opening Hours
Quick answer: The Vatican Museums are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 8:00 pm, with the last entry at 6:00 pm. They are closed on Sundays, except the last Sunday of each month, when entry is free from 9:00 am to 2:00 pm with the last entry at 12:30 pm. The museums also close on a set list of public and religious holidays. The Sistine Chapel is inside the museums, so it keeps the same hours. Everyone has to start leaving the halls about 30 minutes before closing. 


Vatican Museums opening hours are simple once you know the pattern: open six days a week, closed on Sundays, with one free Sunday a month and a handful of holiday closures. 
The part that trips people up is that the official closing time is 8:00 pm but the last entry is two hours earlier, at 6:00 pm, so you cannot turn up at 7:00 pm and expect to get in.
This guide gives you the official 2026 hours in one place: the daily times, the last-entry cut-off, the days the museums are closed, and the free last Sunday. It also covers the Sistine Chapel and St. Peter's, which people often assume runs on the same timing.

The Vatican Museums are open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 am to 8:00 am, all year round. The last entry is at 6:00 pm, two hours before closing, and this is the time that matters when you book. You need to be inside by 6:00 pm, not just on your way.
There is one more timing rule. Staff start clearing the halls about 30 minutes before closing, so from around 7:30 pm you are asked to make your way out. Plan your visit so you are not still deep in the galleries at the last minute.

When the Vatican Museums Are Closed
The museums are closed every Sunday except the last one of the month. On top of that, they close on a set list of public and religious holidays each year. For 2026 the closed dates are:
* 1 January (New Year's Day)
* 6 January (Epiphany)
* 11 February (Vatican City State anniversary)
* 19 March (Saint Joseph)
* 6 April (Easter Monday)
* 1 May (Labour Day)
* 29 June (Saints Peter and Paul)
* 14 and 15 August (Assumption)
* 1 November (All Saints)
* 8 December (Immaculate Conception)
* 25 December (Christmas Day)
* 26 December (Saint Stephen)
If your trip falls on or near one of these dates, plan around it, as the museums are fully closed those days. Dates shift slightly year to year because some depend on Easter, so always confirm against the official calendar close to your trip.
Free Entry on the Last Sunday
On the last Sunday of each month the Vatican Museums open from 9:00 am to 2:00 pm and entry is free for everyone, with last admission at 12:30 pm. It is the one regular day you pay nothing.
The trade-off is crowds. It is the busiest day of the month, you cannot book a skip-the-line ticket for it, and the queue can be very long, so you need patience and an early start. It also does not run when the last Sunday lands on a major holiday such as Easter Sunday, 29 June, or the Christmas period. Sistine Chapel and St. Peter's Hours
People often ask about the Sistine Chapel and St. Peter's separately, so here is how they line up.
The Sistine Chapel is the last room of the Vatican Museums, not a separate site, so it keeps the same opening hours as the museums. You reach it at the end of the route, and because the whole complex clears about 30 minutes before closing, aim to be at the chapel well before the final hour.
St. Peter's Basilica is different. It is not part of the museums and keeps its own hours. The basilica is open daily from 7:00 am, and closes around 7:00 pm from April to September and around 6:30 pm from October to March. 
The dome climb opens at 7:30 am and stops letting people up about an hour before the basilica closes. St. Peter's is also closed to visitors on Wednesday mornings when the Papal Audience is held in the square, reopening around 1:00 pm. Entry to the basilica itself is free. Best Time to Arrive
The first slot at 8:00 am and the late afternoon are the quietest times to go. The middle of the day, from about 10:00 am to 1:00 pm, is the busiest, especially in the peak months from April to October. Weekdays like Tuesday and Thursday are calmer than Saturdays, which are the busiest day of the week.
If you want the museums at their quietest, an early slot is your best bet, and our best time to visit guide goes into the quietest days and seasons in more detail. For how long to set aside once you are inside, see our how long to spend at the Vatican guide.
Frequently Asked Questions
What are the Vatican Museums opening hours?
The Vatican Museums are open from Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 8:00 pm, with the last entry at 6:00 pm. They are closed on Sundays except the last Sunday of the month, which is free from 9:00 am to 2:00 pm with the last entry at 12:30 pm.
Is the Vatican open on Sunday?
No, not on most Sundays. The only Sunday the Vatican Museums open is the last Sunday of each month, when entry is free from 9:00 am to 2:00 pm. St. Peter's Basilica and St. Peter's Square are open on Sundays, but the museums are not.
What time is the last entry to the Vatican Museums?
The last entry to the Vatican Museums is 6:00 pm, two hours before the 8:00 pm closing. On the free last Sunday, the last entry is 12:30 pm. You need to be through the entrance by these times, and the halls start clearing about 30 minutes before closing.
Are the Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel open at the same hours?
Yes. The Sistine Chapel is the final room of the Vatican Museums, so it follows the same opening hours. There is no separate Sistine Chapel ticket or schedule.
What days are the Vatican Museums closed in 2026?
Every Sunday except the last of the month, plus a set list of holidays: 1 and 6 January, 11 February, 19 March, 6 April, 1 May, 29 June, 14 and 15 August, 1 November, and 8, 25, and 26 December.
Can I visit the Vatican Museums at night?
The museums close at 8:00 pm on regular days. On select Friday evenings in the warmer months they run special late openings, which are a separate evening ticket, covered in our Vatican evening and night tour guide. Those aside, there is no standard night entry.
How early should I arrive for my time slot?
Arrive about 15 to 20 minutes before your slot to clear security. In peak season, a queue can build before the 8:00 am opening, so for an early slot it is worth being there ahead of time.
