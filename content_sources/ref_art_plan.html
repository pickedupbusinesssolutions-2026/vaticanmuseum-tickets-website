Colosseum Opening Hours 2026: Times, Last Entry & Holiday Closures

The Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Palatine Hill are open 7 days a week starting at 8:30 am year-round. Closing times vary dramatically by season - from 4:30 pm in winter to 7:15 pm in summer - with strict last entry cutoffs exactly one hour before closing.

Colosseum Opening Hours 2026 Timetable

The Archaeological Park of the Colosseum adjusts its closing times six times per year in coordination with seasonal daylight hours. Entry always commences promptly at 8:30 AM every day of the week. Ensure your Colosseum tickets are pre-booked well in advance to guarantee your preferred entrance time.

Last Entry Policy & Security Timing

Admission strictly terminates one hour before closing time. Turnstiles automatically lock at the last-entry mark, and staff cannot override the entry gates for late arrivals.

⚠️
Arrive 15–30 Minutes Early

Security checkpoints at the Colosseum feature airport-style X-ray scanners and metal detectors. Even with timed skip-the-line reservations, queueing at security can take 15 to 25 minutes during peak hours. If your reservation is for 3:00 PM, plan to be in front of your gate by 2:40 PM.

Roman Forum & Palatine Hill Hours

The Roman Forum and Palatine Hill share the identical 8:30 AM opening time as the Colosseum. However, keep in mind that the monumental gates of the Forum (such as the Via Sacra and Largo della Salara Vecchia entrances) close 30 minutes before the Colosseum's final closing time to ensure all visitors can safely exit the extensive archaeological park before nightfall.

Holiday Closures & Free Admission Days

The Colosseum is open 363 days per year. It closes on only two public holidays:

  • January 1 (New Year's Day): Closed all day.
  • December 25 (Christmas Day): Closed all day.

On Good Friday, the Colosseum closes early around 1:00 PM to prepare for the papal Via Crucis (Way of the Cross) procession led by the Pope.

Free Admission Days (Domenica al Museo)

On the first Sunday of every month, entry to the Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Palatine Hill is free for all visitors under the Italian Ministry of Culture's #DomenicaAlMuseo initiative. Tickets cannot be reserved online for free Sundays; entry is strictly first-come, first-served, and queues frequently exceed 2 to 3 hours.

Colosseum at Night: Evening Hours

Between May and October, the Colosseum opens after dark for exclusive guided night tours (Luna sul Colosseo). These visits take place Thursday through Saturday evenings between 8:00 PM and 11:00 PM (last tour starts at 9:30 PM), offering access to the underground hypogeum and arena floor under atmospheric LED floodlights.

How to Plan Your Day Around the Opening Hours

To make the most of your timed ticket:

  1. The 8:30 AM Slot: Best choice for cooler temperatures, tranquil photography, and shortest security wait times.
  2. The Golden Hour Slot: Booking 2 hours before closing provides dramatic amber light across the ancient travertine arches and lower crowds as day tour buses depart.
  3. Forum First Strategy: If your Colosseum time slot is early afternoon (e.g., 1:30 PM), explore the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill from 10:30 AM to 1:00 PM, take a short break, and head straight to your Colosseum gate.
