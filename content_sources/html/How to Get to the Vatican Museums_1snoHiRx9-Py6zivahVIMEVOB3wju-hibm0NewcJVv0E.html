How to Get to the Vatican Museums

Quick answer: The easiest way to get to the Vatican Museums is by Metro Line A to Ottaviano or Cipro, then a 7 to 10 minute walk to the entrance on Viale Vaticano. Bus 49 stops right in front of the entrance, and a taxi can drop you at the rank in the same square. From Termini station, take Metro Line A straight to Ottaviano, about 15 minutes. Driving is not recommended because parking is limited and much of the area is a restricted traffic zone. When using a map app, search for "Musei Vaticani," not "Vatican City," which sends you to St. Peter's Square instead.

The Vatican sits in the northwest of Rome, in the Prati district, just across the Tiber from the historic center and about a ten minute walk from Castel Sant'Angelo.

It is one of the city's most visited places, so it is well served by public transport, and from most central hotels you can be at the door in well under half an hour.

The metro is the fastest and cheapest way in for most people, but the Vatican is close enough to the center to walk to if you fancy it, and a taxi takes you right to the door.

The best choice really comes down to where you are starting from, so this guide covers each starting point in turn, a central hotel, Termini station, the airport, and a cruise ship at Civitavecchia. For the entrance itself and what to expect at the door, see our Vatican Museums entrance guide.

By Metro

The metro is the fastest and cheapest way to reach the museums. Take Line A (the orange line) to Ottaviano, signed Ottaviano-S. Pietro-Musei Vaticani, or to Cipro. Both are about a 7 to 10 minute walk from the entrance.

From Ottaviano, walk down Via Ottaviano toward the walls, then turn right and follow the wall uphill to the entrance on Viale Vaticano. Cipro is a similar distance from the other side. A single metro ticket is €1.50 and the ride from the center takes around 10 to 15 minutes.

By Bus and Tram

Several buses stop near the entrance:

  • Bus 49 stops in the square directly in front of the museum entrance, the most convenient of all.
  • Buses 32, 81, and 982 stop at Piazza del Risorgimento, about a 5 minute walk away.
  • Buses 492 and 990 stop on Via Leone IV, also a short walk.
  • Tram 19 stops at Piazza del Risorgimento.

A single ticket covers the metro, buses, and trams for 100 minutes, so you can mix them on one fare.

By Taxi

There is an official taxi rank in the square in front of the museum entrance, so a taxi can take you right to the door. Use the official white taxis or a booking app, and ask for "Musei Vaticani" on Viale Vaticano. A ride from the city center is 10 to 20 minutes depending on traffic.

By Train

A regional train station, Roma San Pietro, sits just behind the Vatican, a short walk from St. Peter's Basilica and about 10 to 15 minutes from the museums entrance. Regional lines FL3 and FL5 stop there, which is handy if you are coming from a town outside Rome rather than from the city center.

Cruise passengers docking at Civitavecchia can take a regional train from Civitavecchia to Roma San Pietro, or to Termini and then Metro Line A, a journey of roughly 60 to 80 minutes each way. If you are on a day trip from a ship, leave plenty of time for the return train.

From Termini Station

Termini is Rome's main station and the easiest starting point. Take Metro Line A (direction Battistini) straight from Termini to Ottaviano, with no changes, then walk to the entrance. The whole trip is about 15 minutes plus the walk. A taxi from Termini takes around 20 to 25 minutes depending on traffic.

From the Airport

Neither of Rome's airports connects directly to the Vatican, so you go via the center:

  • From Fiumicino (FCO): Take the Leonardo Express train to Termini, then Metro Line A to Ottaviano. Allow about an hour in total. A taxi has a fixed fare to central Rome and takes 45 to 60 minutes.
  • From Ciampino (CIA): Take a shuttle bus to Termini, then Metro Line A. Allow around an hour and a half with connections.

Airport trains and the Leonardo Express are not covered by a normal city transport ticket, so buy those separately.

By Car and Parking

Driving to the Vatican Museums is not recommended. The streets around the Vatican are narrow and busy, much of central Rome is a restricted traffic zone (ZTL) where driving without a permit means a fine, and there is no visitor car park at the museums.

If you do drive, use a paid garage and book it ahead rather than hunting for street parking, which is scarce and restricted. A few options within walking distance of the entrance:

  • Parking Risorgimento, at Piazza del Risorgimento, the closest to the entrance at about a 5 to 10 minute walk.
  • Aurelia Parking Vaticano, just southwest of the Vatican, a short walk or one bus stop away.
  • Auto Park Garage Clodia, near Clodio and the Cipro metro stop, about a 10 to 15 minute walk.

Rates vary by garage and how long you stay, and several can be reserved online in advance through a parking site such as Parclick, which is worth doing in busy periods. For most visitors, though, the metro or a taxi still works out faster and cheaper than driving and parking.

Walking to the Vatican Museums

The Vatican is walkable from much of central Rome. From St. Peter's Square, follow the Vatican wall north and uphill for about 10 to 15 minutes to reach the museums entrance, as the two are not in the same place.

From the Piazza Navona area it is a pleasant 25 to 30 minute walk across the river. Wear comfortable shoes, as you have a lot more walking ahead of you inside.

Once you arrive, skip the ticket queue with a pre-booked skip-the-line entry ticket, and check our opening hours and best time to visit guides to time your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the nearest metro station to the Vatican Museums?

Ottaviano (signed Ottaviano-S. Pietro-Musei Vaticani) or Cipro, both on Metro Line A. Each is about a 7 to 10 minute walk to the entrance on Viale Vaticano.

How do I get to the Vatican Museums from Termini?

Take Metro Line A from Termini straight to Ottaviano, with no changes, then walk to the entrance. The trip is about 15 minutes plus the short walk.

Which bus goes to the Vatican Museums?

Bus 49 stops in the square right in front of the entrance. Buses 32, 81, and 982 and tram 19 stop at nearby Piazza del Risorgimento, about 5 minutes away.

How do I get to the Vatican Museums from the airport?

From Fiumicino, take the Leonardo Express train to Termini, then Metro Line A to Ottaviano, about an hour in total. From Ciampino, take a shuttle bus to Termini, then the metro.

Can I drive and park at the Vatican Museums?

Driving and parking are not recommended at the Vatican Museums. There is no visitor car park, parking nearby is limited, and much of central Rome is a restricted traffic zone. Use a paid garage near Piazza del Risorgimento if you must drive, or take the metro or a taxi instead.

Why does my map app take me to the wrong place?

Searching "Vatican City" points to St. Peter's Square, which is a 10 to 15 minute walk from the museums. Search "Musei Vaticani" or "Vatican Museums entrance" to be routed to the right door on Viale Vaticano.
