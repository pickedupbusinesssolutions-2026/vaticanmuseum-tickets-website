Visitor Tips

What You Need to Know Before Visiting

📅

When to Book

Book at least 2–3 weeks in advance during peak season. Specialized Colosseum underground tours and arena floor guided tours sell out within days of release. Don't risk arriving without a ticket.

🎒

What to Bring

Bring your booking confirmation (digital or printed), a valid photo ID, comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen, and a refillable water bottle. No large bags or tripods allowed.

🔐

Security Checks

All visitors pass through airport-style security. Allow an extra 15–20 minutes even with skip-the-line tickets. Prohibited items include selfie sticks, food, and beverages.

♿

Accessibility

The Colosseum is partially accessible for wheelchair users. Elevator access is available to the 1st and 2nd levels. Some underground sections are not fully accessible.

💡
Pro Tip: Arrive 10 Minutes Early

Even with pre-booked Colosseum skip-the-line tickets or an official guided tour, arrive 10 minutes before your time slot to allow for security checks. Groups depart on time - latecomers may be left behind.

FAQ

Colosseum Tickets: Frequently Asked Questions

The cheapest option is the official entry ticket at €16 (adult), which includes Roman Forum and Palatine Hill. However, this requires waiting in the regular queue which can be 2–4 hours long. For EU residents aged 18–25, the price is just €2. Children under 18 from EU countries enter free. If your time is valuable, a skip-the-line ticket from €29 is far better value when you factor in the time saved.
During off-peak months (November–March), same-day tickets may be available. In peak season (April–October), attempting to buy on the day is a major risk. The Colosseum has a daily visitor cap, and all time slots typically sell out days or weeks in advance. We strongly recommend booking at least 1 week ahead and ideally 3–4 weeks ahead for peak travel periods.
The arena floor is where gladiators fought - a reconstructed wooden floor over the hypogeum that gives a dramatic ground-level view of the stadium. The underground hypogeum is the network of tunnels and chambers beneath the arena where animals, gladiators, and equipment were kept before shows. Both require special access tickets beyond standard entry. Our Guided Tour (from €49) includes both.
Most skip-the-line and guided tour tickets offer free cancellation up to 24–48 hours before the tour date. Official Colosseum tickets purchased directly from the site are generally non-refundable. When booking through our recommended providers, look for the "Free Cancellation" badge on the ticket listing to ensure flexibility for your travel plans.
