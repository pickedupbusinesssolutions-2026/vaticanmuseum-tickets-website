Practical Advice

Insider Tips for an Unbeatable Visit

Maximize your time and avoid common pitfalls with advice from seasoned Rome travel experts.

👟

Sturdy Footwear is Essential

The ancient basalt paving of the Via Sacra and the Colosseum's marble stairs are worn, uneven, and slippery. Wear supportive, closed-toe walking sneakers.

💧

Refill at Historic Nasoni Fountains

Carry a refillable water flask. Cold, fresh mountain drinking water flows continuously from traditional cast-iron fountains (nasoni) throughout the Forum and park.

🧭

Reverse Your Route in Morning

The Colosseum security line is busiest from 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM. Enter Palatine Hill first at 8:30 AM when gates are quiet, and schedule your Colosseum slot for early afternoon.

📱

Download Offline Maps & Audio

Cellular data reception can be weak inside the thick brick vaults of the Colosseum and down in the Forum ruins. Download digital tickets and guides beforehand.

☀️

Plan Around Minimal Shade

The Roman Forum and Arena Floor have virtually no tree canopy or roof cover. In warmer months, bring a brimmed hat, sunglasses, and high-SPF sunscreen.

🎟

24-Hour Forum Flexibility

You do not need to rush into the Forum immediately after the Colosseum. Your archaeological park pass allows entry anytime within 24 hours of first validation.

Frequently Asked Questions About What to See

Everything you need to know about planning your sightseeing itinerary across the Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Palatine Hill.

If you hold a ticket or tour with Arena Floor access, head onto the arena surface first to enjoy the monumental low-angle view before the midday sun heats up. For standard ticket holders, start on Level 1 for the grand exterior gallery view, move up to Level 2 for the museum and elevated photography, and then proceed through the Arch of Titus into the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill.

The Arena Floor is the reconstructed wooden platform on the arena's surface where gladiator duels took place, offering an awe-inspiring open view up at 48 metres of seating tiers. The Underground (Hypogeum) is the subterranean stone corridor network located beneath the arena floor, where fighters and wild animals were kept in holding bays before being raised through trapdoors. Both require specialized guided tour bookings.

Yes. Every standard or guided Colosseum ticket automatically grants entry to both the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill. While your Colosseum entry time is strictly scheduled, your access to the Forum and Palatine Hill remains valid for 24 hours from your initial ticket validation.

The Attic refers to the restored 4th and 5th tiers at the summit of the amphitheatre rim. Reached via a modern glass panoramic elevator at the Sperone Valadier entrance, it offers a dramatic bird's-eye view from 50 metres above Rome. Access requires a dedicated "Full Experience Attic" ticket or an authorized Attic tour.

No. Due to strict archaeological conservation and visitor safety regulations, independent unguided exploration of the Hypogeum is strictly forbidden. All visitors must be accompanied by an accredited official guide, and tour group sizes are capped at 24 participants.

Yes. The Colosseum Museum located on the inner ring of Level 2 is open to all ticket holders at no extra cost. It features scale models, original Roman marble decorations, gladiator equipment, and multimedia displays detailing the amphitheatre's medieval and modern history.
