25 Best Things to Do in Rome in 2026: The Ultimate Guide

From ancient ruins to Renaissance art, hidden trattorias to rooftop bars - everything you need for an unforgettable Rome trip. Written by our Rome-based travel experts.

  1. Home
  2. ›
  3. Best Things to Do in Rome

Rome is one of the world's greatest cities - a place where ancient history, Renaissance art, baroque fountains, and incredible food all exist side by side. Whether it's your first visit or your fifth, there's always something new to discover.

We've lived in Rome and explored it from corner to corner to bring you this definitive list of the 25 best things to do in Rome in 2026 - from the iconic to the unexpected.

🏛 Ancient Rome & Historic Landmarks

1

Visit the Colosseum

Rome's most iconic landmark - the ancient amphitheater where gladiators once fought. Visit the underground hypogeum for the full experience. Book skip-the-line tickets well in advance.

🎟 Book Colosseum Tickets →
2

Explore the Roman Forum & Palatine Hill

Included with your Colosseum ticket - walk through the heart of ancient Rome. The Forum was the political and commercial center of the Roman Empire. Allow at least 1.5 hours.

🎟 Book Combo Ticket →
3

Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel

Home to Michelangelo's breathtaking ceiling fresco, the Vatican Museums are a must-visit. Book early access tickets to see the Sistine Chapel before the crowds arrive.

🎟 Book Vatican Tickets →
4

St. Peter's Basilica & Dome Climb

The grandest church in the world is free to enter. Climb the dome (551 steps or elevator partway) for panoramic views over Rome. Arrive early or late afternoon to avoid peak crowds.

5

Pantheon

The best-preserved ancient Roman building in the world, dating to 125 AD. The oculus (open hole in the dome) creates a stunning light effect. Since 2023, a small entry fee applies.

⛲ Fountains, Piazzas & Architecture

6

Throw a Coin at the Trevi Fountain

The world's most famous fountain - and the legend says throwing a coin guarantees your return to Rome. Visit early morning (before 8:00 AM) for photos without thousands of tourists.

7

Piazza Navona

One of Rome's most beautiful squares, home to Bernini's spectacular Fountain of the Four Rivers. Perfect for sitting at a café, watching street performers, and soaking up Roman life.

8

Spanish Steps

The iconic 135-step staircase between Piazza di Spagna and the Trinità dei Monti church. Come at sunrise for peaceful views and the best photos.

🎨 Art, Culture & Museums

9

Borghese Gallery

One of the world's greatest art museums, with Bernini's breathtaking marble sculptures and Caravaggio paintings. Limited to 360 visitors per 2-hour session - book months in advance.

🎟 Book Borghese Tickets →
10

Capitoline Museums

The world's oldest public museums, home to Marcus Aurelius's original bronze statue, the Capitoline Wolf, and stunning views over the Roman Forum.

🍕 Food, Wine & Local Experiences

11

Eat Carbonara in Testaccio

Testaccio is Rome's original food neighborhood and birthplace of many classic Roman dishes. Try authentic carbonara, cacio e pepe, and supplì (fried rice balls) here.

12

Take a Rome Food Tour

The best way to experience Roman cuisine - a guided food tour takes you to historic markets, local bakeries, and family-run restaurants the guidebooks don't mention.

13

Try Gelato from a Local Gelateria

Avoid touristy gelato with huge mounds of brightly colored ice cream - look for gelato stored in covered metal containers, which indicates artisan quality.

🌅 Viewpoints & Hidden Gems

14

Sunrise at the Janiculum Hill

The best panoramic view in Rome, overlooking the entire city. No crowds at dawn - just you, a coffee, and one of the most spectacular cityscapes in the world.

15

Peek Through the Knights of Malta Keyhole

Rome's most unusual viewpoint - a perfectly framed view of St. Peter's Basilica through a keyhole. Queue is usually short but it's worth every second.

16

Trastevere Neighborhood Walk

Rome's most charming neighborhood - cobblestone streets, ivy-covered buildings, and some of the best trattorias in the city. Best explored in the evening.

17

Campo de' Fiori Market

A lively morning market in one of Rome's most atmospheric squares. Browse local produce, flowers, and street food. By evening, the square transforms into a buzzing social hub.

🛍 Shopping & Day Trips

18

Via Condotti & Via del Corso Shopping

Via Condotti for high-end luxury shopping (Gucci, Valentino, Bulgari), or Via del Corso for more accessible Italian fashion brands.

19

Day Trip to Ostia Antica

A stunning ancient Roman port city just 30 minutes from Rome by train - and far less crowded than Pompeii. An incredible, underrated experience.

20

Day Trip to Tivoli (Villa d'Este & Hadrian's Villa)

Just 30km from Rome - Tivoli offers two UNESCO World Heritage sites in one day trip. The Renaissance gardens of Villa d'Este and the vast ruins of Hadrian's Villa are extraordinary.
